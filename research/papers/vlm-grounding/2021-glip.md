## Core paper explanation

GLIP reformulates object detection as aligning image regions to words in a prompt. Deep fusion lets visual and language features influence one another, so category names become grounded language inputs rather than a fixed classifier vocabulary.

## Execution flow

Follow the data through the mechanism. Training objectives and inference behavior are distinguished in the linked explanations.

### Fuse image and language

Let $O\in\mathbb R^{N\times d}$ describe candidate regions and $P\in\mathbb R^{M\times d}$ prompt tokens. A cross-attention update has the form
$$\operatorname{Attn}(O,P)=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{(OW_Q)(PW_K)^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)PW_V.$$
The softmax runs over valid text tokens. GLIP repeatedly fuses modalities; this equation explains an attention operation, not the entire fusion block.

### Align regions to phrases

Alignment scores are $S=OP^\top\in\mathbb R^{N\times M}$. A positive map $Y_{ij}$ identifies tokens referring to region $i$, including multi-token phrases. Training combines alignment classification and localization:
$$L=L_{\mathrm{align}}(S,Y)+L_{\mathrm{loc}}(\hat b,b).$$
Preserve the implementation's positive-map normalization and focal/localization losses; a phrase cannot always be represented by one token.


**Planned component problems:** Build Phrase-to-Token Positive Maps; Aggregate Token Scores into Region Scores. These are planned work, not runnable links; titles without a reviewed blueprint remain unreviewed ideas.

### Ground a prompt

A predicted box $b=(x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2)$ must use one declared pixel or normalized image convention. Compare predicted and annotated regions with $\operatorname{IoU}=|b\cap b^*|/|b\cup b^*|$. A valid phrase match also requires the correct object identity, not only box overlap.


**Prerequisite practice:** [Compute Pairwise Bounding-Box IoU](/problems/box-iou-broadcast). These supporting skills do not complete this model stage.

## Why this matters on a robot

Grounding can connect “the red mug” to a camera region. A correct 2D region still needs depth, calibration, and a manipulation policy. Ambiguous referring expressions and unseen object relations are important failure slices.
