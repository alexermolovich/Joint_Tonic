## Core paper explanation

iMAP represents a scene with a neural network and jointly estimates camera poses and the map from RGB-D observations. Rendering predicts color and depth, allowing tracking and mapping to use the same scene representation.

## Execution flow

Follow the data through the mechanism. Training objectives and inference behavior are distinguished in the linked explanations.

### Query the implicit scene

For world location $x\in\mathbb R^3$, the scene network predicts $(c(x),\sigma(x))$. Sample a camera ray $x_k=o+t_kd$ with metric depth $t_k$. Camera pose determines how each pixel ray enters the world map.




**Planned component problem:** Query Color and Density from a Coordinate MLP. This is an unreviewed implementation idea for the mechanism above, not a runnable exercise; its precise contract and evaluator must be reviewed before publication.

### Render color and depth

For interval $\Delta_k$, define
$$\alpha_k=1-e^{-\sigma_k\Delta_k},\quad w_k=\alpha_k\prod_{j<k}(1-\alpha_j),$$
$$\hat C=\sum_k w_kc_k,\qquad \hat D=\sum_k w_kt_k.$$
These volume-rendering equations explain the shared prediction mechanism. Sampling and uncertainty treatment determine practical tracking quality.


**Implement this component:** [Compute Transmittance and Alpha Compositing Weights](/problems/nerf-alpha-compositing). The reusable volume-compositing component accepts densities and finite metric intervals. It does not implement iMAP tracking, keyframe selection or model optimization.

### Track and update the map

RGB and depth residuals compare $(\hat C,\hat D)$ with measured values. Tracking fixes the map while updating the current pose; mapping revisits selected keyframes and network parameters. Information-guided pixel selection focuses limited rendering work on useful observations.


**Planned component problems:** Sample Informative Mapping Pixels; Optimize a Camera Pose Against a Neural Map; Choose Keyframes by Reconstruction Error. These are planned work, not runnable links; titles without a reviewed blueprint remain unreviewed ideas.

## Why this matters on a robot

An implicit map offers a compact continuous representation. Plausible completion of unseen surfaces is a learned inference, not an observation; collision systems must distinguish measured support from hallucinated geometry.
