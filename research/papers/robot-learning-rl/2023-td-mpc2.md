## Core paper explanation

TD-MPC2 learns a latent dynamics model, reward predictor, value ensemble, and policy prior for online model-predictive control. It combines short-horizon latent planning with terminal value estimates and stabilizing representation choices.

## Execution flow

Follow the data through the mechanism. Training objectives and inference behavior are distinguished in the linked explanations.

### Learn normalized latent dynamics

Encode $z_t=h(o_t)$ and predict $\hat z_{t+1}=d(z_t,a_t)$. A consistency term compares $\hat z_{t+1}$ with a stopped-gradient encoding of $o_{t+1}$. SimNorm partitions a vector into groups and applies softmax within each group, so each group's entries sum to one.


**Implement this component:** [Roll Out a Latent Dynamics Sequence](/problems/tdmpc2-latent-dynamics-sequence).

**Implement this component:** [Apply Groupwise SimNorm to Latent States](/problems/tdmpc2-groupwise-simnorm).


### Fit rewards and values

A TD target has the form $y=r+\gamma(1-d)\bar Q(z',a')$, with target ensemble estimates and policy-prior actions. Reward/value prediction uses discrete regression in transformed space rather than relying only on raw squared error. The stochastic policy prior uses maximum-entropy learning.


**Implement this component:** [Build Distributional Reward and Ensemble-Value Targets](/problems/tdmpc2-distributional-ensemble-targets).


### Plan and execute one action

The planning score is
$$J(a_{0:H-1})=\sum_{t=0}^{H-1}\gamma^t\hat r(z_t,a_t)+\gamma^H\hat V(z_H).$$
Sample-based optimization uses the policy prior to inform candidates, executes the first action, and replans. A terminal value reduces finite-horizon bias but can introduce critic extrapolation error.


**Implement this component:** [Score Candidate Action Trajectories](/problems/tdmpc2-candidate-trajectory-scores).

**Implement this component:** [Update a Sampling-Based Planner](/problems/tdmpc2-weighted-elite-planner).

## Why this matters on a robot

Latent planning can reduce expensive pixel-space prediction. Poor latent coverage, model exploitation, and stale observations remain important failure cases in contact-rich control.
