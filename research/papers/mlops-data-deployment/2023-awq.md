## Core paper explanation

AWQ performs activation-aware weight-only quantization. It identifies channels important to layer outputs through activation statistics and rescales weights to reduce quantization error without relying on mixed-precision protection of individual weights.

## Execution flow

### Inspect activation importance

For a linear layer $Y=XW$, weight error $\Delta W$ produces output error $\Delta Y=X\Delta W$. Large activation channels amplify corresponding weight errors. This explains why weight magnitude alone is not an adequate importance measure.

### Search equivalent channel scales

For positive diagonal scale $S$,
$$\hat Y=(XS^{-1})Q(SW).$$
Without $Q$, this equals $XW$. AWQ searches scaling choices using calibration activation statistics and output error, with clipping choices in its quantization recipe. It does not require gradient-based model retraining.

### Serve low-bit weights

A useful layer diagnostic is $\|XW-\hat Y\|_F^2/\|XW\|_F^2$ when the denominator is nonzero. This is a teaching error measure, not a substitute for task evaluation. Weight-only compression leaves activation precision and runtime kernels as separate design choices.
