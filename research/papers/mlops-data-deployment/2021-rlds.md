## Core paper explanation

RLDS treats a dataset as episodes containing ordered steps, together with metadata. This preserves information that flat observation/action pairs can discard. The central engineering problem is semantic consistency across collection, transformation and consumption; RLDS is not a new policy architecture or loss function.

## Execution flow

### Record lossless episode structure

As an original schema formalization, let a dataset be

$$
\mathcal D=\{E_i\}_{i=1}^{N},\qquad
E_i=(m_i,[s_{i,0},\ldots,s_{i,T_i-1}]).
$$

$m_i$ is episode metadata, $s_{i,t}$ a structured step and $T_i$ its recorded length. Steps preserve observations, actions, rewards, discounts and boundary flags when defined. **is_last** means the recording ends; **is_terminal** means the environment reached a terminal state. A time limit can make the first true and the second false.

[Episode Schema Validation](/problems/validate-robot-episodes) is an implemented prerequisite with a separate teaching schema. **Version a Cross-Robot Dataset Manifest** is planned. These names do not imply the current validator implements every RLDS field.

### Align observations actions and rewards

Under the paper's SAR alignment, an action at step t leads from observation $o_t$ to $o_{t+1}$ and is associated with reward $r_t$:

$$
(o_t,a_t,r_t,\gamma_t,o_{t+1}),\qquad 0\le t<T_i-1.
$$

$\gamma_t$ is a dimensionless discount. Observations/actions carry their own sensor frames and units; t is an ordered step index, not necessarily a timestamp. In the final SAR step, only the observation and related metadata are defined. Converting to another alignment requires shifting fields and updating which endpoint values are defined.

| Recorded step | Observation | Outgoing action/reward | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | o0 | a0,r0 | is_first |
| Middle | o1 | a1,r1 | neither |
| Last | o2 | Undefined in SAR | is_last |

[Joint/Camera Interpolation](/problems/interpolate-joint-state) is a timestamp-alignment prerequisite. It does not decide reward/action alignment. **Window an Episode Without Crossing Boundaries** is planned.

### Build reproducible training views

A full H-step training window starting at s is admissible only if

$$
0\le s,\qquad s+H\le T_i,
$$

with a separate field-validity rule if final steps lack outgoing targets. [Sliding Action Chunks](/problems/act-sliding-action-chunks) provides an implemented single-episode target-window exercise. Do not equate its action-array length with every RLDS episode's observation count.

A useful operational identity is a versioned view key

$$
k=\operatorname{hash}(\text{source snapshot},\text{transform version},\text{configuration}).
$$

This is a teaching lineage formalization, not a paper-defined cryptographic protocol. [Record Dataset, Transform, Model, and Evaluator Lineage](/problems/record-artifact-lineage) is now a runnable provenance component.

## Why this matters on a robot

A reset boundary must not become a valid transition between unrelated scenes. Treating every truncated episode as terminal also changes bootstrap targets. Retain clock provenance, calibration, action units, embodiment identifiers and source licenses alongside standardized fields.

## Implement the components

Existing validation, alignment and chunking problems are prerequisites. A complete RLDS-compatible converter requires a reviewed schema and lossless round-trip tests; no original dataset is redistributed by this card.
