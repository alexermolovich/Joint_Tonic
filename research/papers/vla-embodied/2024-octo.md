## Core paper explanation

Octo combines a transformer with modular observation/task tokenizers and a diffusion action head. The model is designed to transfer across robot setups by adapting interfaces while retaining a useful pretrained representation. Named token groups and attention rules make missing modalities and new inputs explicit. Its open generalist policy is more than a backbone: data mixtures, action normalization, and adaptation choices are part of the method.

## Execution flow

Task and observation groups are tokenized, masked attention produces policy readouts, and a conditioned diffusion head generates action chunks. Adaptation can add or replace interfaces for new observation/action spaces.

### Tokenize available modalities

Let group $g$ provide $n_g$ tokens $E_g\in\mathbb R^{n_g\times d}$ and availability mask $m_g$. The transformer input is the concatenation of available groups with their temporal and positional metadata. A validity mask must be distinguished from a numeric feature of zero: a present zero joint value is a valid measurement; an absent sensor is not.

$$
E=[E_{\mathrm{task}};E_{\mathrm{obs},1};\ldots;E_{\mathrm{readout}}].
$$

[Episode schema validation](/problems/validate-robot-episodes) provides a related bounded check before this tokenization stage.

### Apply group-aware attention

For queries $Q$, keys $K$, and values $V$, use an additive attention mask

$$
M_{ij}=\begin{cases}0,&\text{allowed pair},\\-\infty,&\text{forbidden pair},\end{cases}
\qquad Z=\operatorname{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k}+M)V.
$$

Temporal causality, group visibility, and padding determine the allowed pairs. A readout token summarizes information for the action head without requiring every observation tokenizer to know the final action dimension. Prevent an all-masked query row or define a safe fallback; its softmax is otherwise undefined.

### Generate a conditioned chunk

For action chunk $A^{(0)}$ and readout $z$, a noise-prediction diffusion head can be described by

$$
A^{(k)}=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_k}A^{(0)}+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_k}E,
\qquad\mathcal L=\mathbb E\|E-\epsilon_\psi(A^{(k)},k,z)\|_2^2.
$$

[Add Noise to an Action Sequence](/problems/diffusion-action-noising) implements the shared forward mechanism, not Octo’s complete architecture. At adaptation time, input tokenizers or output heads may change; the normalization, mask, and action-dimension contracts must change with them. A parameter-count check alone does not establish successful transfer.

## Why this matters on a robot

Modular interfaces reduce the work of adapting a pretrained policy, but hidden sensor assumptions can survive fine-tuning. Test absent modalities, timing differences, and changed action bounds. Report both adaptation data/compute and closed-loop performance rather than comparing only training losses.
