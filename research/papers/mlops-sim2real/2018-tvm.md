## Core paper explanation

TVM separates high-level tensor computation from hardware-specific execution schedules. Graph transformations simplify and combine operations, while schedule choices determine loop ordering, tiling, memory use, and parallel execution. Automated optimization searches for schedules that run well on a target device without changing the mathematical tensor operation.

## Execution flow

Represent tensor computation, choose legal schedules, and measure generated implementations on the target hardware.

### Specify tensor semantics

For matrix multiplication with $A\in\mathbb R^{M\times K}$ and $B\in\mathbb R^{K\times N}$,

$$
C_{ij}=\sum_{k=0}^{K-1}A_{ik}B_{kj}.
$$

The reduction axis differs from output axes. A compiler representation must preserve bounds, dtypes, layouts, and reduction semantics before attempting performance changes.


**Component practice**

- Fuse Compatible Operators in a Compute Graph — unreviewed idea; contract and evaluator required.

### Schedule loops and memory

A tile factor $T$ decomposes index $i$ into $i=T i_o+i_i$, with $0\le i_i<T$. When $M$ is not divisible by $T$, the final tile needs a boundary predicate. Reordering a floating-point reduction can change roundoff even when the real-number formula is unchanged; tolerances and permitted transformations must be explicit.


**Component practice**

- Search Tensor Tile Sizes with a Toy Cost Model — unreviewed idea; contract and evaluator required.
- Reuse Tensor Buffers with Liveness Analysis — unreviewed idea; contract and evaluator required.

### Search and benchmark schedules

For legal schedule set $\mathcal S$, select

$$
s^*=\arg\min_{s\in\mathcal S}\operatorname{Latency}(s;\mathrm{device},\mathrm{shape},\mathrm{dtype}).
$$

Warmup, synchronization, allocation, and measurement scope affect this value. A schedule tuned for one shape or accelerator is not automatically optimal for another. Retain correctness evidence alongside the timing record.


**Component practice**

- Compare Schedules with Reproducible Latency Measurement — unreviewed idea; contract and evaluator required.

## Why this matters on a robot

Edge compute budgets make kernel layout and scheduling practical concerns. An impressive isolated kernel result may disappear once camera preprocessing, data transfer, and control-loop synchronization are included. Pin compiler, driver, and hardware settings for reproducible measurements.
