# Expansion to 150 distinct research papers

Acquired: 2026-11-13 (America/Denver)

## Scope and counting

This expansion adds **119 distinct primary-source papers** to the prior **31 distinct papers**, for **150 canonical papers total**. There are **151 local PDFs** because the existing corpus contains two copies of Open X-Embodiment in different topic folders. Those existing files were preserved. This is a curated implementation library, not an exhaustive bibliography or a quality ranking.

Each new PDF has a same-basename Markdown acquisition card with identity metadata, an original method summary, three practical component candidates, a visual teaching plan, operational extensions, and canonical links. These concise cards were checked against primary identity/abstract metadata; they explicitly require full-text technical review before a problem blueprint becomes implementation-ready. They do not contain wholesale paper transcriptions or unverified headline metrics.

## Balanced primary-topic allocation

Each canonical paper has exactly one primary counting bucket; cross-topic use remains appropriate. Existing Open X-Embodiment counts once under VLA/generalist policies; GR00T N1 counts under humanoids; robomimic counts under data/evaluation.

## Discovery and source policy

Discovery used the existing audits of [Awesome Robotics](https://github.com/ahundt/awesome-robotics) and [YanjieZe Paper-List](https://github.com/YanjieZe/Paper-List), together with foundational methods needed to fill topic gaps. Every downloaded expansion artifact resolves to an official arXiv record and author-deposited PDF. Secondary indexes were not used as the authority for titles, authors, dates, or technical claims. The year in a new filename is the primary record's submission year; final publication venues are deliberately not guessed. Current PDF bytes are pinned by SHA-256 even when an unversioned canonical URL later changes.

Open access for reading does not automatically permit public rehosting. Preserve the source and verify paper, dataset, code, model, and asset rights separately before redistribution.
