# Progressive hints

## Hint 1

Draw the joint axis and the vector from its origin to the tool. Both must use the same frame.

## Hint 2

A revolute joint contributes tangential linear velocity and angular velocity. A prismatic joint contributes linear motion only.

## Hint 3

Build columns independently, then stack them using the declared linear-first row order. Verify a single joint at a known positive rate before testing a mixed chain.
